Scope: the whole S2C app (dashboard, trade screen, stats, journal, settings). Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one trader practising gold blind; places orders, watches rules, reviews results.
Constraints: Turkish UI, desktop only, no emoji, custom icons, original sounds, no filler copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Performance as a graded certificate. Every account and every closed trade is issued a label, like a graded coin slab; funding is the moment the slab is sealed. Refuses the default near-black terminal with one neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: Smoked-acrylic warm graphite ground in three plies; ivory label stock (cert strips) carrying ink type; gold foil only for certification and primary action; teal-green / coral-red at matched lightness for direction; one holographic security strip that exists only on funded labels. Archivo throughout: normal width for UI, condensed caps for label heads, expanded heavy for the S2C mark. Tabular figures everywhere.

STORY: The trader sees their accounts as issued certificates, opens one, trades blind with rules visible as gauges, and at session end the label prints the truth (real date and price).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard. Left 64px icon rail with S2C hallmark. Top: account label strips in a row, each ivory cert strip (account no., size, status grade); the three rule gauges sit on a dark ply footer under each strip, because their risk colours (amber, coral) need the dark ground to read. Below: equity history and hour x weekday heatmap side by side, payouts table. Primary action "Yeni hesap" gold, top right.

FORM: Candidate 4 of 7, coin-grading slab label system. seed:f0450e9ac37e18ae
Raise (from ticket wallet): states stamp themselves; nothing disappears, it cancels — violated accounts and cancelled orders stay visible, stamped.
Raise (from star atlas): one fixed magnitude ramp for hierarchy — trade markers and heatmap cells sized/tinted on one ramp by R.
Signature interaction: label printing — session-end reveal and funding seal print onto the ivory strip; the holo strip shimmers under the pointer.
